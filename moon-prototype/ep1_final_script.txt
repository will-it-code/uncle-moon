=== S1_GREETING (0:00-0:30) ===
Hello, little star. Yes, you, warm under your blanket. I have been
waiting for you, up here in my quiet sky. The night is soft tonight.
The stars are sleepy. And I have a story for you. A small story, about
a very small light. Are you cozy? Good. Then close your eyes a little,
and let us begin.

=== S2_MEADOW (0:30-1:30) ===
Below me, in the meadow, the grass is swaying, slow and silvery in the
moonlight. And there... do you see her? A tiny light, floating low over
the flowers. That is Pippa. Pippa the firefly. Her light is not big
like mine. It is small, and warm, and it flickers, like a little candle
in the wind. Every night, Pippa flies through the meadow, sharing her
glow with anyone who needs it. She loves to help. It is her favorite
thing in the whole wide night.

=== S3_WORRY (1:30-2:00) ===
But tonight... tonight something is wrong. Pippa's light is flickering
faster. It sputters. It dims. Oh no. Pippa is afraid. What if her little
light goes out? What if she cannot help anyone anymore? She sits on a
leaf, and her glow grows smaller... and smaller...

=== S4_BIRD (2:00-2:25) ===
High in the old oak, a baby bird is crying. He is afraid of the dark.
Pippa flies up, up, up, and hovers beside the nest. Do not be afraid,
she whispers, and she glows, soft and steady. The baby bird watches her
little light... and his eyes grow heavy... and he sleeps. But Pippa's
glow is a little dimmer now.

=== S5_TURTLE (2:25-2:50) ===
Down by the pond, Tommy the turtle is lost. The water is dark, and he
cannot find his way home. Pippa zips through the reeds, her light
swinging like a tiny lantern. Follow me, she hums. And Tommy follows
the little glow, all the way home. But Pippa's light is dimmer still.
It flickers... sputters...

=== S6_DIM (2:50-3:15) ===
I can do it, Pippa says. I can light the whole meadow myself. She
rises high, and glows with all her might. But her little light...
wavers... and fades... until it is almost gone. Poor Pippa. She sinks
to the grass, in the dark.

=== S7_GLOWBACK (3:15-4:00) ===
Then... a flutter. The baby bird wakes, and flies down to her. Tommy
lumbers over, his shell shimmering with pond-light. One by one, the
meadow friends gather round, and each shares a little glow. The light
flows back into Pippa, warm and golden. And oh! She glows brighter than
ever before. Steady. Strong. Pippa laughs, a tiny sparkling laugh. She
did not have to shine alone. None of us do.

=== S8_RITUAL (4:00-4:30) === [REWRITTEN Oct 16, 2026 — viewer anchor]
And you, little star... yes, you. You have a little light too. Every
time you are kind, every time you share, it glows a little brighter.
Put your hand right here... can you feel it? Warm, like a tiny candle.
That is yours. Keep it safe tonight. Remember what we always say...
Story ends, friendship stays. Goodnight, little star. Sleep soft.

=== S9_TEASE (4:30-5:00) ===
But wait... do you hear that? Far away, at Moon Lake... splash. Did you
see it? A silver tail, slipping under the water. Who could it be? Hmm.
Tomorrow night... we shall find out. Sleep now, little star. I will be
right here, until morning.
